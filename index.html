      UIL Spelling rules (Section 1470)

      A summary for students and parents. The official Constitution and Contest Rules win if anything differs.

      The contest

      • Designed to expose students to a wide variety of vocabulary — not a memorization contest.
      • Words come from the UIL list, state-adopted textbooks and words of common usage. About 20% of test words are not on the UIL list.
      • The authority for all words is Merriam-Webster's Intermediate Dictionary.
      • Students write the words given by the pronouncer at about five words per minute.
      DivisionTestTie breakerApprox. length
      Grades 3 & 450 words15 words30 min

      The tie breaker is given to all contestants right after the test. Students who don't take the tie breaker are disqualified.

      What counts as a miss

      • Any misspelled word — one point is deducted for each.
      • A mistake in capitalization, or misuse of an apostrophe or hyphen.
      • Any word the student leaves out (blank).
      • Where the list shows two spellings (like nosy, nosey), either one is correct. A word listed only in lowercase must be lowercase; one listed only with a capital must be capitalized.

      Printing or cursive are both fine; a letter counts if it can be identified on its own. In this app, your typed answer is checked exactly — capitals, hyphens, spaces and apostrophes included. (Accent marks like the é in éclair are optional when typing.)

      The pronouncer

      • Pronounces words in order, distinguishing homonyms (for example “bass drum” vs. “base”).
      • May give a sentence and/or definition for clarification.
      • Students may not ask for a word to be repeated or for a sentence — the pronouncer decides that beforehand. Choose Contest rules mode to practice this way.
      • After all words are read, the pronouncer and verifier re-pronounce any words in question.

      Scoring & ties

      • Highest score is first place, and so on through sixth.
      • Ties are broken by scoring the tie breaker of only those tied. If still tied, the tie stands. A tie for first means no second place, etc.
      • Students who score 100% at district get a Certificate of Achievement. Tie breaker misses don't count against it.
      • Team score = the combined scores of a school's three contestants (tie breaker only if teams are tied).
